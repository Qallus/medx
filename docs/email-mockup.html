Needs your call

Three things before this goes live

  • Is the subject line right?

    Currently New inquiry — Jordan Avery · Alcohol Detox. The service is appended only when the visitor picked one, so it stays scannable in a crowded inbox.

  • Should anyone else be copied?

    It goes to Kyra alone right now. A second recipient or a shared inbox is a one-word change, and it is the cheapest insurance against a single mailbox filtering these.

  • Does the confidentiality line stay?

    The footer notes that the message contains information submitted in confidence. Fitting for a clinic, but it is your call whether it belongs on an internal notification.
