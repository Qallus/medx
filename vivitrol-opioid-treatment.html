Medication & treatments

A month of protection, per shot

Vivitrol blocks the effects of opioids for about a month per injection — powerful relapse protection for the vulnerable period after detox, with no daily medication to manage.

Request an appointment Call 480-219-0055
How it works

A full block, renewed monthly

Vivitrol is extended-release naltrexone: it occupies opioid receptors and blocks them completely. If opioids are used, they simply don’t produce the expected effect — which steadily unlearns the craving cycle.

Because it’s an antagonist, Vivitrol isn’t an opioid, isn’t habit-forming, and isn’t a controlled substance. For many patients it’s the right fit after detox — especially when a daily medication feels like one more thing to manage.

Starting requires being opioid-free for roughly 7 to 14 days. That window is exactly what our supervised detox is built for — we manage it safely, then begin injections at the right moment.

  • Blocks opioid effects for about a month
  • Non-addictive, non-controlled medication
  • We manage the required detox window first
  • Strong protection through early recovery
